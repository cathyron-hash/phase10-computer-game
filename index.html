How to play

Rearrange your cards by dragging one onto another. Tap cards to select them for a phase, hit, or discard.

After you have laid down your own phase, select one card from your hand and tap “Hit with selected card” under a group it fits. You must keep at least one card to discard.

Each turn, draw one card, then lay down a valid phase if you can, hit on a laid phase if eligible, and discard one card. Play a Skip from your hand by choosing a target; it waits in front of that player and moves to the discard pile when their turn is skipped.
